— Our Story

Why We Built This

The Indian internet is full of coding resources. YouTube channels with millions of subscribers. Udemy courses on deep discount. Bootcamps promising "₹10 LPA jobs guaranteed." And yet, thousands of students still don't know where to start, what to learn in what order, or why half of what they're learning doesn't matter for actual jobs.

We've seen 12th graders open three YouTube tabs, get overwhelmed, and close them all. We've seen engineering students learn React before they understand how the internet works. We've seen people buy five courses and finish none of them because nobody told them the right sequence.

"Ek 10th pass baccha bhi yahan se Full Stack Developer ban sakta hai. Step by step. Free. Real. No shortcuts."

— Core Promise, Coodley

Coodley was built to fix that. Not with hype. Not with certificates that mean nothing. Not with paywalls that punish people who can't afford them. With an honest, detailed, sequenced curriculum that tells you exactly what to learn, why, in what order — and doesn't waste your time.

We are India's most honest developer learning platform. That's a claim we take seriously. Every page title is exactly what's inside. Every lesson is written for real understanding — not just syntax copying. And we will never charge you for the core content.

The Problem We Solve

Most Indian students don't fail because they're not smart. They fail because they learn in the wrong order, from unstructured sources, without understanding the "why." We fix the sequence.

Honest About AI

— Design Philosophy

How We Build Every Page

These aren't just internal rules. They directly affect your learning experience.

No Clickbait Titles

Every page title is exactly what's inside. "JavaScript Async" is about async JavaScript. "Pointers in C++" covers pointers and memory. Period.

Mobile + Desktop

Built mobile-first. Tested on actual budget Android phones. If it doesn't work at 360px, we don't ship it.

Hinglish Where It Helps

We write in English with Hindi mixed in where it genuinely helps understanding — especially for technical concepts with tricky mental models.

We tell you how long each topic actually takes to understand — not to "complete," which is meaningless. DSA takes months. React takes weeks. We say so.

Clear Fork Points

The roadmap has explicit fork points — Web Developer path vs Software Engineer path. We explain the trade-offs at every fork so you can choose with information.

Dark + Light Mode

Your preference, saved locally. Never resets. Designed carefully for both modes — not just inverted colors. Your eyes, your choice.

— AI Reality 2026

We're Honest About AI Too

Most platforms ignore this. We don't. AI is changing what developers need to know — and we give you the real picture.

AI can write boilerplate code. It can explain syntax. It can generate unit tests. That's real, and it's already here. Pretending otherwise would be dishonest.

But AI cannot architect a system from scratch. It cannot debug a complex distributed system failure. It cannot understand your specific business context. It cannot replace someone who deeply understands how and why systems work.

That's why we still teach DSA. That's why System Design is on the roadmap. And that's why we have a dedicated section that tells you exactly what to focus on — based on data, not guessing.

Read our full AI Reality report: What AI can and cannot do in 2026 → Includes specific action plans for Indian developers.

AI Can Do This
  • Write boilerplate code
  • Explain syntax & errors
  • Generate unit tests
  • Autocomplete repetitive tasks
  • Summarize documentation
  • Debug simple logic errors
AI Cannot Do This
  • Architect complex systems
  • Debug distributed failures
  • Understand business context
  • Make product decisions
  • Lead engineering teams
  • Solve truly novel problems

The verdict

Developers who use AI well are already outcompeting those who don't. But AI-native developers who also understand fundamentals — DSA, architecture, systems thinking — are the ones no one can replace.

— FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Real questions. Real answers. No PR fluff.

Is Coodley really free? Koi hidden charge nahi hai?
Yes. All core content — every lesson, every roadmap, every guide — is free. Forever. We may add optional community features or tools in the future that have a cost, but the learning content will always be free. No bait-and-switch.
I'm in 10th standard. Is this too early to start?
Not at all. CS Fundamentals and the Terminal module are specifically designed for absolute beginners with zero background. Starting in 10th or 11th gives you a massive head start. You don't need to learn everything — just start with CS Basics and move at your own pace.
Which language should I learn first — C++, Java, or Python?
It depends on your goal. Python if you want the easiest start or are interested in AI/ML. C++ if you want to do DSA, competitive programming, or want deep control of memory. Java if you're targeting campus placements at Indian IT companies or enterprise software. We cover all three — check the Full Roadmap for the fork point explanation.
Do I need a laptop, or can I learn from my phone?
All content is readable and fully functional on mobile. However, for actually writing and running code, you'll eventually need a computer. A basic laptop (even 8GB RAM, any decent processor) is enough. You can use online editors like Replit or CodeSandbox in the browser if you're stuck with a phone temporarily.
Will Coodley give me a certificate I can add to my resume?
Honestly? Our certificates aren't something most interviewers will care about. What they care about is your GitHub projects, your DSA performance, and your ability to explain your decisions. We'll help you build all three. We'll also tell you which certifications are actually worth your time (AWS, GCP etc) and which ones aren't.
Mujhe pehle se thoda coding aata hai. Kahan se start karoon?
Check the Full Roadmap and identify where your knowledge drops off. If you know HTML/CSS but not JavaScript well — start there. If you know JavaScript but haven't done React — start React. If you've never done DSA seriously, start there. The roadmap will help you find your gaps.
How is Coodley different from W3Schools, freeCodeCamp, or GeeksforGeeks?
Those are great resources. The differences: Coodley gives you a complete, sequenced path in the correct order (most resources are reference docs, not curricula). We're India-specific in context, language, and career advice. We have a dedicated AI Reality section. And we're more opinionated — we tell you what to skip, not just what exists.
How do I contact you or report an error?
Email us at hello@coodley.in. For legal or privacy matters: legal@coodley.in. For content errors, we credit everyone who reports a genuine mistake. We respond to every email — usually within 48 hours.
